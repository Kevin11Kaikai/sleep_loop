Preregistered Route-3 7D Single-round NPE Ensemble¶

Three independently initialized MAF density estimators use the same independent 4,096-row bank, fixed train/validation split, and training-only scaler. Proposal is the frozen seven-dimensional prior. The equal-weight mixture is transparent; there are no sequential rounds or held-out-driven adjustments.

Training histories and validation-only diagnostics¶

Validation loss, boundary accumulation, finite/support rate, and ensemble disagreement are engineering diagnostics. They do not determine the formal scientific verdict, which remains locked until independent held-out validation.
