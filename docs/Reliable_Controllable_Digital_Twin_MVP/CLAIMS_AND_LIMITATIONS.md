# Claims and Limitations

## Highest supported combined claim

> **Bounded in-silico reliable and controllable mechanistic digital twin MVP.**

The integration supports the terminal label `RELIABLE_AND_CONTROLLABLE_DIGITAL_TWIN_MVP` through `C1_CONTROLLABILITY_MVP_PASS_T2_PRIMARY`.

## Supported

- C006 was selected from a prospectively defined 16-candidate bank using the frozen FIT rule.
- C006 was frozen before HELDOUT evaluation and before C1 controller confirmation.
- The frozen C006 protocol, model, and selection provenance are hash-consistent across all six fresh C1 runs.
- M1 held-out evidence supports a narrow, proxy-level, one-subject mechanistic consistency claim: C006 HELDOUT NRMSE 0.247845 versus 0.463775 for matched C000, with 46.5592% relative improvement.
- Frozen F01 is causal and uses the registered additive cortical actuator with amplitude 0.045 inside the legal range [0, 0.05].
- All six C1 trajectories were finite, causal, engaged, within legal bounds, below 10% duty, hash-consistent, and passed registered integrity/safety gates.
- The primary T2 response was positive in 3/3 fresh seeds at 220 s and 3/3 at 910 s.
- Median dT2 was +0.1913% at 220 s and +0.1784% at 910 s.
- I_SO independently corroborated the primary direction in 3/3 seeds at both durations.
- The primary response survived the prospectively fixed 910 s duration gate.

## Visible secondary trade-offs

- `I_SP` was adverse in 3/3 seeds at both 220 s and 910 s.
- `T6` was positive in only 1/3 seeds at each duration, with slightly negative medians (-0.000941 and -0.001398).
- `P_C_GIVEN_SP` was positive in 2/3 seeds with positive medians, but remains a conditional-event diagnostic.
- These outcomes support the interpretation **“primary controllability demonstrated with secondary dynamical trade-offs,”** not simultaneous optimization of all sleep dynamics.

## Not supported

- Clinical efficacy or safety.
- Patient benefit.
- Validated therapeutic control.
- Population generalization or cross-subject validity.
- Night-2 prediction or sealed clinical validation.
- Human causal mechanism.
- Global controllability of C006.
- Simultaneous optimization of all sleep metrics.
- Resolution of all spindle/coupling trade-offs.
- A claim that the model proxy is voltage, scalp EEG, or a validated forward-model output.

## Terminology boundary

- **Reliable** is used only for the frozen, independently reproduced M1 evidence chain within its one-subject Night-1, four-band, dimensionless proxy scope.
- **Controllable** is used only for the prospectively registered T2-primary response under frozen F01, fresh seeds, matched SHAM, legal actuator limits, and numerical integrity gates.
- **MVP** indicates closure at this bounded evidence level; it does not convert an in-silico response into a therapeutic claim.
