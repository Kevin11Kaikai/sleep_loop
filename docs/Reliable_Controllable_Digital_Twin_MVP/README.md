# Reliable + Controllable Digital Twin MVP

## One-sentence result

M1 established why C006 can serve as the frozen reliable mechanistic digital twin; C1 then prospectively demonstrated that the same frozen C006 exhibits a causal, bounded, reproducible, and duration-robust primary control response.

## Package purpose

This directory is a minimal, dissertation-ready evidence archive. It connects two already-closed authoritative campaigns without rerunning simulations, refitting C006, retuning F01, or reopening either scientific decision.

The highest supported combined claim is:

> **Bounded in-silico reliable and controllable mechanistic digital twin MVP.**

## Evidence chain

1. **M1 - Reliable:** sixteen mechanistic candidates were compared on 110 FIT epochs. C006 had the lowest FIT NRMSE (0.254612), was selected, and was frozen before HELDOUT access.
2. **M1 - Credibility:** on 110 HELDOUT epochs, frozen C006 achieved NRMSE 0.247845 versus 0.463775 for matched non-personalized C000, a 46.5592% relative improvement. The paired epoch MSE advantage had a 95% bootstrap interval of [0.079800, 0.101465].
3. **Freeze boundary:** M1 protocol, model, and selection hashes were retained in every C1 result; all six fresh C1 runs matched the M1 hashes.
4. **C1 - Controllable:** frozen causal F01 produced a positive primary T2 response in 3/3 fresh seeds at both 220 s and 910 s. Median dT2 was +0.1913% and +0.1784%, respectively; I_SO was supportive 3/3 at both durations.
5. **Bounded conclusion:** primary controllability is established under the registered in-silico protocol, while spindle/coupling trade-offs remain visible.

## Interpretation guardrail

“Reliable” means frozen, independently reproduced, proxy-level mechanistic consistency within the M1 one-subject Night-1 scope. “Controllable” means a prospectively frozen primary T2 response to a legal, causal, bounded F01 intervention in the same frozen in-silico model. Neither term implies clinical efficacy, patient benefit, population generalization, global controllability, or simultaneous optimization of every sleep dynamic.
