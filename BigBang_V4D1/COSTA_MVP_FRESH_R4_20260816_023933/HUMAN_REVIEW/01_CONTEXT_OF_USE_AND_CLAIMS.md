# Context of use and claims

This development-only evaluation asks whether a FIT-selected candidate from a frozen 16-candidate thalamocortical model bank improves disjoint Night-1 N3 proxy-feature prediction over frozen matched nonpersonalized C000 for one public subject, SC4001.

The observable is a four-element vector of dimensionless log10 relative power for slow-oscillation, delta, theta, and spindle bands. The model observable is not voltage, microvoltage, a validated scalp forward model, or a direct biological measurement.

Assigned claim: `M1 / C0 / P0 / DISCOVERY_PLUS_INDEPENDENT_REPRODUCTION`. Reproduction used blind independent execution of the same frozen code, data, and environment, not an independent implementation.

Maximum wording: “Model-internal personalized mechanistic consistency was independently reproduced for a frozen proxy-level one-subject Night-1 discovery analysis.” No human causal mechanism, population generalization, empirical wrong-subject specificity, Night-2 prediction, sealed validation, clinical efficacy, treatment/stimulation effect, medical-decision use, or dissertation sufficiency is established.
