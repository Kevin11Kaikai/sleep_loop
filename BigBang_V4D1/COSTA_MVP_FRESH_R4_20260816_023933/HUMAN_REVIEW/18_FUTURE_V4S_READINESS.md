# Future V4S readiness

R4 is closed for the narrow M1/P0 objective; it does not itself authorize a broader campaign. Any future upgrade would require a new prospective protocol, fresh role and access isolation, a new raw/protected budget, and explicit authorization before access.

Scientifically, broader readiness would require multiple subjects, a population-estimated comparator, serial-dependence-aware uncertainty, an independently implemented reproduction, empirical specificity tests, a validated observation/forward model, and separately sealed or Night-2 evaluation as applicable.

These are future requirements, not claims achieved by R4. Current statuses remain C1 `NOT_ASSIGNED` and M2/C2/P1/P2 `NOT_ELIGIBLE/NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
