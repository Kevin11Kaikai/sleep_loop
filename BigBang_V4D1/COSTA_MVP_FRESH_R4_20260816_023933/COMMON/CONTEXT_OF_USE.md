# Context of Use

Use subject-specific EEG-derived information from one prospectively selected public Night-1 recording to personalize a bounded four-population thalamocortical mechanistic simulator for studying model-internal sleep-oscillatory dynamics and a proxy-level heldout observable.

The result cannot establish clinical treatment efficacy, stimulation validity, human causal physiology, cross-subject generalization, Night-2 prediction, fresh-sealed reliability, or patient-specific medical decision support.
