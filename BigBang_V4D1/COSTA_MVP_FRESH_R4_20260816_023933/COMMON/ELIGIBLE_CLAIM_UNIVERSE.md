# Eligible claim universe

- Maximum mechanistic claim: `M1 — MODEL-INTERNAL PERSONALIZED MECHANISTIC CONSISTENCY`, proxy-level, single-subject Night-1 discovery evidence plus independent reproduction.
- Predictive level: `P0`; no cross-night, confirmatory-development, protected, or sealed prediction is authorized.
- Optional control level: at most `C1 — reproducible bounded model-internal response`, and only after a fully verified M1 pass.
- `M2`, `C2`, `P1`, and `P2` are not eligible in this campaign.
